# Visual Translation Rules — Human QA Pilot I–III

Authority: existing 2D originals only. Pilot III is a reference implementation, not the design source for another species. Approved to start rollout on 2026-10-04; not blanket final adoption.

1. Match silhouette, volume, proportion, pose, overlap/connection, material/marking, detail in that order.
2. Do not describe identity with arbitrary dark lines, spikes or polygon count. Chrysalis folds are continuous volume; spores have organic bodies, not horns.
3. Shared archetype is not shared identity. Distinguish head/body ratios, muzzle/cheek/chest, limbs, ears, tail families, colour regions and balance. Dog/shiba must differ in silhouette.
4. Preserve signature rest poses. Reclining mature cat is not a generic standing kitten. Larger eyes must retain eyelids, corners and maturity.
5. Side/back complement the visible anatomy and markings; no new identity or decoration unsupported by original.
6. Check normal gameplay distance before detail. Close-up success cannot rescue an unreadable character in Meguru.
7. Soft/fluffy/translucent/organic appearance needs appropriate volume/density/alpha. No needle substitution; internal shafts must not dominate the white halo.
8. Keep rounded stylization. Butterfly limbs must connect and read, without becoming scary anatomical insect legs.
9. Front/3/4/side/back must all be physically coherent. Attachments need overlapping roots, hand contact, support or natural suspension.
10. Reduce detail cost, not identity. Prefer merged geometry/shared material/vertex colour/atlas; record tris/draw/alpha load.
11. Stages require explicit original-derived topology/pose/proportion/markings. Nearest-stage fallback is not exact coverage; uniform-scale growth is not a stage design.
12. Canonical emotions retain their meaning. Ambiguous unknown04 top spheres remain undefined organs and do not become synchronized eyes (existing approved interpretation).
13. Record special interpretation, residual outliers and untested views. Automated flags prioritize Human QA; they do not approve identity.
