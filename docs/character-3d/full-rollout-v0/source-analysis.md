## Coral representative continuation
Original player-3 strip and coral05 PNG re-read at71539b8. Stage02 has a low peach bulb, radial slender pink rounded-tip tentacles, purple stone support and closed-happy normal eyes. Stage05 is a wide asymmetric orange-red antler colony, thick rooted trunk/core, rounded fork tips, warm stone support and left closed/right open normal eyes. Side/back are rounded continuous tubes with root overlap, not flat billboards. Branch paths are explicit; tip globules remain anatomy, never extra faces.
Reuse: one face-bearing merged body, common Rig/materials/canonical face, existing plantSway. Branching is a small builder extension for future rooted organisms; no state, new locomotion semantics or per-tip actor. Representative gates precede remaining coral stages.
