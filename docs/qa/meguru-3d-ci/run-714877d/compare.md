## Visual assessment

- home15/45: complete flower groups replace the clipped groups at the road edge. home64 retains one complete safe bed. home196 shows grouped planting beside the existing stepping-stone route.
- Party sprites and speech labels obscure portions of the entrances in these normal game views; static plan evidence supports footprint checks but cannot replace rendered visual review.
- Small houses still have tall narrow wall masses; the main-roof head-clearance contract prevents repeating the previous unsafe vertical compression.
- Countryside retains open roads, low clusters and the existing farm grammar. The isolated farmhouse reveals veranda posts ending without a connected roof. That is the next bounded silhouette/entrance improvement.
- Forest/jungle/city are controls in this repair; no new regional-composition improvement is claimed.
- Human QA remains pending for iPhone ghosts/afterimage/player disappearance/stutter/p95/p99/>60ms and final beauty. No World completion or production-ready judgment.
